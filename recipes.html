Easy Pasta Recipes

Three simple recipes that are inexpensive, flexible, and easy to repeat.

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

About 20 minutes · Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • Parsley, salt, and Parmesan to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
  2. Warm olive oil in a pan and gently cook the garlic.
  3. Add red pepper flakes and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Toss in the spaghetti and finish with parsley and Parmesan.

Fettuccine Alfredo

About 25 minutes · Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz fettuccine
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • Black pepper and salt to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the fettuccine until al dente.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and add the cream.
  3. Stir in Parmesan over low heat.
  4. Add pasta and a small splash of pasta water until smooth.

Recipe update: use 1 cup heavy cream 3/4 cup heavy cream for a lighter sauce.

Penne Arrabbiata

About 25 minutes · Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz penne
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup crushed tomatoes
  • Red pepper flakes, salt, and parsley

Steps

  1. Cook the penne in salted water.
  2. Cook garlic and chili flakes briefly in olive oil.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for about 10 minutes.
  4. Toss with the cooked pasta and garnish with parsley.
