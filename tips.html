Simple Pasta Cooking Tips

A few habits make basic pasta taste much better without adding much work.

1. Salt the water

Add salt after the water starts boiling. The pasta absorbs some of the seasoning while it cooks, so this is your first chance to build flavor.

2. Save some pasta water

Before draining, save about half a cup. The starch in the water helps sauce cling to the pasta and can loosen a sauce that becomes too thick.

3. Use a simple ratio

A simple beginner formula is 8 oz pasta : 2 servings. It is not programming code here, but the tag is useful for presenting a short formula-like snippet in a distinct way.

4. Finish the pasta in the sauce

Move the pasta into the sauce for the last minute of cooking. Add a little pasta water and toss until the sauce coats the noodles.
